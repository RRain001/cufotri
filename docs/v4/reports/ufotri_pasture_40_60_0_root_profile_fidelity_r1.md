Stage: `UFOTRI_PASTURE_40_60_0_ROOT_PROFILE_FIDELITY_R1`

## 1. Result

D2 adds a separate opt-in grass/pasture root-distribution profile that maps an explicit total plant-water uptake flux to the three established soil layers as `40/60/0`. The existing nutrient/root-crop `20/40/40` UFOTRI profile and the root solver are not modified.

## 2. Evidence boundary

The repository's frozen V2 parameter-source audit records:

`grass_root_fractions,crops.parameters.grass.root_fraction_by_layer,0.4;0.6;0.0,...,manual direct,UFOTRI manual,grass root fraction table,...,A,accepted`

The same audit separately records root-crop `0.2;0.4;0.4` as manual direct. The two distributions are therefore treated as different manual roles rather than competing global defaults.

D2 uses the stage label "pasture" because the grass role belongs to the grass/pasture-to-cow food-chain route selected in Phase D. This does not validate or activate project-specific pasture LAI, biomass, cutting schedule, dormant LAI, root-activity fraction or feed assumptions.

## 5. Machine qualification

The D2 machine gate and independent pytest coverage require all of the following:

1. exact pasture fractions `(0.4, 0.6, 0.0)`;
2. sum of fractions equals one;
3. existing crop fractions remain `(0.2, 0.4, 0.4)`;
4. for `q_total=4e-8 m/s`, pasture layer fluxes are exactly `(1.6e-8, 2.4e-8, 0)`;
5. pasture and crop profiles preserve the same total water uptake;
6. pasture L3 uptake is exactly zero;
7. layer allocation differs from the crop profile;
8. under equal soil-water HTO concentration, profile selection does not change total HTO transfer;
9. total pasture water transfer equals `q_total * dt`;
10. each layer obeys `J=qC`;
11. under unequal layer concentrations, total HTO transfer equals the explicit sum of layer `q*C*dt` contributions;
12. water ledger closes;
13. HTO ledger closes;
14. the existing interval-start forcing contract is preserved.

Fail-closed tests additionally cover negative, NaN, infinite and wrong-unit total uptake inputs.

## 6. Why equal-concentration comparison is used

Changing root fractions can legitimately change total HTO uptake when layer HTO concentrations differ. D2 therefore does not impose an incorrect invariant that 20/40/40 and 40/60/0 must always have equal HTO transfer.

Instead, the equal-concentration case isolates the allocation operation: with identical layer concentration and identical total water uptake, the two profiles must have identical total HTO transfer. A separate unequal-concentration case verifies the physically expected profile-dependent transfer while enforcing `J=qC` and conservation.

## 7. C1/C2 compatibility

D2 changes only root-layer allocation. Signed inter-layer water/HTO hydrology remains generated by C1/C2 and projected through the existing soil forcing contract. The root profile continues to enter through the existing root forcing boundary, so no hydrologic flux is reinterpreted or double counted.

The selected `PRESERVE_V3_POST_OBT_ROOT_GAIN` operator ordering is unchanged.
