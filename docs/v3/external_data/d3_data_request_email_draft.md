Dear EMRAS/KAERI soybean scenario data custodian,

I am conducting a traceable review of plant tissue-free-water tritium
modelling and would like to ask whether the original source data for the EMRAS
soybean scenario can be shared for research use.

The public scenario material refers to three workbooks that I could not
retrieve from the current IAEA pages:

- `sbTbox.xls` (glove-box air HTO);
- `sbTHS.xls` (temperature, humidity and solar/light conditions);
- `sbWR.xls` (wind and rain).

If available, I would also be grateful for sample-level plant-body and
organ-level TFWT observations, exact exposure and sampling timestamps,
post-exposure background HTO, replicate/composite membership, fresh and dry
mass, analytical uncertainty and detection limits, instrument/method
information, a data dictionary, missing-value and quality-flag definitions,
and any correction/version notes.

Could you also state the applicable licence, citation requirement and whether
the files or derived tables may be redistributed? Native workbooks plus
lossless CSV exports would be ideal. Original precision, units, timestamps and
quality flags will be preserved.

The files would initially undergo only checksum, licence and data-quality
review. They would not be used for calibration or formal model validation
until a separate independent evidence-registry approval is complete.
